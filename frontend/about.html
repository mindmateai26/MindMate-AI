ABOUT MINDMATE AI

Project Purpose & Technical Architecture

A student-friendly, full-stack mental wellness screening, preliminary ML risk prediction, and multilingual conversational companion.

⚠️
Academic & Ethical Disclaimer: MindMate AI is developed strictly as an academic engineering demonstration. It is NOT a certified medical device, does not provide psychiatric diagnoses, and must not replace professional clinical consultation or crisis interventions.

1. Project Overview & Objective

Many students and young adults experience academic strain, sleep disruptions, and social isolation without accessible, stigma-free initial screening. MindMate AI bridges this awareness gap by offering:

  • A preliminary 9-indicator self-assessment evaluating daily mood, academic stress, sleep, and executive functioning.
  • An explainable Machine Learning prediction pipeline categorizing risk into Low, Mild, Moderate, or High tiers with factor transparency.
  • A culturally nuanced conversational companion fluent in English, Tamil, and colloquial Thanglish.
  • Emergency crisis intervention triggers that immediately direct vulnerable individuals to verified national mental health helplines.

2. Full-Stack Technology Stack

Backend Core

Python 3.11+, FastAPI REST API, Pydantic V2 validation, Uvicorn ASGI server.

Machine Learning

Scikit-Learn Random Forest Classifier, Pandas, NumPy, Joblib model serialization.

Generative AI

Official Google GenAI Python SDK (google-genai), Gemini 2.5 Flash, contextual session memory.

Frontend Architecture

Vanilla HTML5, modern CSS3 with custom variables, modular JavaScript, responsive card UI.

3. AI/ML Pipeline & Explainability

Rather than functioning as an unexplainable "black box", MindMate AI evaluates 9 explicit self-reported wellness dimensions:

mood_rating, stress_level, sleep_quality, concentration_difficulty, academic_work_pressure, social_withdrawal, energy_level, anxiety_frequency, and daily_functioning_impact.

The Random Forest model calculates the contribution weights of each dimension. If an individual scores high on Academic Pressure and Sleep Disruption, the system explicitly lists these as the top drivers of their score and provides targeted stress-regulation strategies.

4. Multilingual & Gemini Conversational Companion

The companion assistant incorporates:

  • Conversational History Retention: Maintains multi-turn context throughout the active session (e.g., remembering names, stressors, and exam dates).
  • Linguistic Empathy: Understands English, native Tamil (தமிழ்), and mixed Thanglish expressions (such as "enaku romba stress ah iruku" and "manasu valikuthu").
  • Emotion Tagging: Detects emotional valence (Sadness, Stress, Anxiety, Anger, Loneliness, Academic pressure, Neutral) and attaches supportive tags to responses.
  • Resilient Architecture: Automatically routes to Gemini API when a valid API key is present in backend/.env, and provides an offline-capable conversational engine when disconnected.

5. System Limitations

  • Non-Clinical Scope: Screening scores and risk tiers are preliminary and statistical, not medical diagnoses.
  • Self-Report Bias: Results rely entirely on self-reported inputs which may fluctuate with daily mood.
  • Emergency Protocol: If acute distress is detected, the AI companion ceases normal conversation and instructs the user to seek human contact and call 14416 or 112 immediately.
