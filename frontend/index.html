If you or someone you know is in distress, having thoughts of self-harm, or facing an acute crisis, please contact these verified, free, and confidential helplines immediately:

📞 1800-599-0019
24/7 Helpline for anxiety, depression, and psychological crisis

📞 +91 44 2464 0050
24-Hour Confidential Helpline
National Emergency Services:
📞 112
