🚨 Immediate Crisis & Helpline Support

If you or someone you know is in distress, having thoughts of self-harm, or facing an acute crisis, please contact these verified, free, and confidential helplines immediately:

Tele-MANAS (Govt of India Mental Health Helpline):
📞 14416 / 1800-891-4416
24/7 Toll-Free, Multi-lingual support including Tamil & English
KIRAN Helpline (Govt of India):
📞 1800-599-0019
24/7 Helpline for anxiety, depression, and psychological crisis
Sneha Suicide Prevention Centre (Tamil Nadu):
📞 +91 44 2464 0050
24-Hour Confidential Helpline
National Emergency Services:
📞 112
Immediate Police / Medical emergency response

MindMate AI is an AI assistant, not a clinical emergency service. Please reach out to trusted loved ones or medical personnel.
